# Sapphire target-selection decision

The reconstruction target is the Japanese origin retail release, product code `AXPJ`, software revision 0. It is the highest-priority locally observed release. Its 8 MiB size, hashes, and decoded header are recorded in `config/target.json`.

English `AXPE` revisions 0, 1, and 2 were observed but remain comparison candidates. The selected status is `identified`, not `verified`, because local hashes and header integrity are not independent provenance corroboration or a reproducible matching build. No ROM bytes are stored.
